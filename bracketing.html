Part 1 — Computations 15 points

Formulas used and computed values for each tournament format.

1. Single elimination, N = 16 Total games = N − 1 = 16 − 1 = 15 games
Rounds = ⌈log₂N⌉ = ⌈log₂16⌉ = 4 rounds
2. Single elimination byes, N = 10 Bracket size = next power of 2 ≥ N = 16
Byes = Bracket size − N = 16 − 10 = 6 byes
3. Double elimination, N = 16 Minimum games = 2N − 2 = 2(16) − 2 = 30 games
Maximum (with bracket reset) = 2N − 1 = 31 games
4. Double elimination, N = 10 Minimum games = 2N − 2 = 2(10) − 2 = 18 games
Maximum (with bracket reset) = 2N − 1 = 19 games
5. Single round robin, N = 10 Games = N(N − 1) / 2 = 10(9) / 2 = 45 games
Rounds = N − 1 = 9 rounds (5 matches per round)
6. Double round robin, N = 10 Games = N(N − 1) = 10(9) = 90 games
Rounds = 2(N − 1) = 18 rounds
7. Stepladder, N = 6 Games = N − 1 = 6 − 1 = 5 games

5 games total. The two lowest seeds play first; the winner climbs the ladder to face the next-highest seed, and so on, until the top seed enters at the championship game.
